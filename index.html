Currently building

Evogen — a generational life-sim in Godot. When your character dies, you don't restart: you inherit their legacy and keep playing as their descendant. Traits, mutations, and past choices carry forward across generations.

Right now: building the data layer — a Character resource per life, and a Bloodline manager tracking the family tree.
